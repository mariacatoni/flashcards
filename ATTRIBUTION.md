# Data attribution

## HSK syllabus

Vocabulary level, headword, official pinyin, and part-of-speech information
originate from the *Syllabus for the Chinese Proficiency Test*, published by
the Center for Language Education and Cooperation in November 2025 and marked
effective July 2026.

- Official syllabus page: https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus
- Full official PDF: https://hsk.cn-bj.ufileos.com/3.0/%E6%96%B0%E7%89%88HSK%E8%80%83%E8%AF%95%E5%A4%A7%E7%BA%B21219.pdf

The source rows used here were extracted by the MIT-licensed
`Punpuf/hsk-syllabus-vocabulary-parser` project at commit
`2adf7c9885a17077966922b89de7ce655d7d82a0`.

- Project: https://github.com/Punpuf/hsk-syllabus-vocabulary-parser

CC-CEDICT, maintained by MDBG and community contributors.

- Project: https://cc-cedict.org/

## Hanzi Writer character data

Stroke-order coverage is checked against the `chanind/hanzi-writer-data`
repository. That data originates from Make Me a Hanzi and Arphic fonts and is
distributed under the Arphic Public License. The current coverage report is
pinned to repository commit `68d10a4b21150cae5e1ebbd223eed289cf32d90c`.

- Data repository: https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data
- License notice: https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data/blob/master/ARPHICPL.TXT
